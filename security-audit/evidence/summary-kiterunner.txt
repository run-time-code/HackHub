found:
  GET 200  /api//health
  GET 200  /api//Health
  GET 200  /api//stats
  GET 200  /api//Stats

Only the public health/stats routes answered 200; all authenticated routes
correctly returned 401. Express routing is case-insensitive by default,
hence the /Health and /Stats duplicates.
